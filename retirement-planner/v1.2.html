How this is worked out

Modelled year by year in today's dollars, using the same published default assumptions as the Australian Government's Moneysmart retirement planner: investment returns net of investment fees and tax, typical super fund administration fees and insurance premiums, 12% employer super, 15% contributions tax, and the Age Pension income and assets tests (rates from 20 March 2026, thresholds from 1 July 2026) including deeming of financial assets.

Growth & inflation: salaries and wages grow 3.7% p.a., prices (CPI) 2.5% p.a. Results are deflated to today's dollars by wage growth until retirement and CPI after (the Moneysmart approach). Cash outside super earns 4.3%, shares/managed funds 7.0%, investment property grows 3.7% p.a. with rent as your entered yield on current value; the property is kept (never sold) and counts in the assets test.
Super fees & insurance: while working — $59 + 0.11% of balance admin fee (capped at $710) and $599 p.a. insurance; in retirement — $69 + 0.14% admin fee (capped at $900). Dollar amounts indexed to CPI.
Contributions: employer 12% of salary; employer and salary-sacrifice contributions taxed 15%; dollar contributions keep pace with wages. Contribution caps and the transfer balance cap are not modelled (a warning appears if your contributions exceed the $32,500 concessional cap for 2026–27).
Drawdown: from retirement, super moves to an account-based pension and is drawn to meet your target income after Age Pension, rent and other income — at least the legislated minimum drawdown for your age. Any excess is banked to savings. If super runs out, cash then shares are drawn before income falls to the Age Pension alone.
Age Pension: maximum rates $31,223 p.a. (single) / $47,070 p.a. (couple combined) from 20 Mar 2026, indexed to wages. Assets test: full pension below $333,000 (single homeowner) / $499,000 (couple homeowner), $600,000 / $766,000 for non-homeowners, reducing $3 per fortnight per $1,000 above. Income test: free area $226 / $396 per fortnight, 50c per dollar taper, with financial assets deemed at 1.25% up to $66,800 (single) / $110,600 (couple) and 3.25% above (1 July 2026 settings, indexed to CPI). Personal effects of $25,000 are included in the assets test. Pension age is 67. In couple mode the pension is scaled while only one of you has reached 67, and retirement income is measured from the year the second person retires.
This is a general-information tool, not financial advice — it doesn't consider your personal circumstances, and the assumptions (based on the Australian Government's Moneysmart retirement planner defaults) won't match any fund exactly.
Actual returns vary year to year; Age Pension entitlements depend on your full circumstances at the time. Consider talking to a licensed financial adviser before acting.
